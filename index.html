About Me

The analyst behind the insights

I am a Business Analyst with an academic background in Business with Logistics and Supply Chain Management and experience spanning operational, healthcare and business-focused environments.

My approach to business analysis centres on understanding stakeholder needs, identifying process gaps, documenting clear requirements and translating business problems into structured, practical solutions. Through my project work, I have developed artefacts such as business requirements documents, stakeholder matrices, RACI matrices, process maps, business cases and performance analysis dashboards.

My experience across different working environments has also strengthened my understanding of communication, accountability, risk awareness and the importance of well-defined processes. I aim to bring these perspectives together to help teams understand problems clearly and make better-informed decisions.
